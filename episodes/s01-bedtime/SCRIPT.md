## Fact card (on-screen text only)
**BEDTIME PROCRASTINATION**
Going to bed later than you planned, when nothing is stopping you.
*Kroese et al., 2014, Frontiers in Psychology*

### Fact check (Nov 24, 2026)
- The term and its definition come from a peer-reviewed paper: Kroese, De Ridder, Evers & Adriaanse (2014),
  "Bedtime procrastination: introducing a new area of procrastination", *Frontiers in Psychology* 5:611.
  The paper defines it as going to bed later than intended with no external circumstances accounting for the delay.
- The "revenge" explanation (staying up because the day left no free time) is widespread in popular
  sources (Sleep Foundation, Healthline), but there is no strong study behind it. So it is **Mika's own
  line (L3), not a claim on the fact card.**
